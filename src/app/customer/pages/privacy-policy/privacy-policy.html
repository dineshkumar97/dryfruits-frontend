Welcome to NutNest

This Privacy Policy explains how NutNest collects, uses, stores and protects information when you visit our website or place an order.

1. Information We Collect

When you use our website, we may collect:

  • Your name and mobile number.
  • Email address.
  • Delivery address and location details.
  • Order details and purchase history.
  • Information you submit through our contact form.

2. How We Use Your Information

We may use your information to:

  • Process and deliver customer orders.
  • Provide order updates and customer support.
  • Respond to enquiries and complaints.
  • Improve our products and website experience.
  • Maintain business and transaction records.

3. Payment Information

NutNest currently offers payment options displayed during checkout. Any future online payment integration will use an appropriate payment service provider. We will not claim to process or store card details unless such a service is actually implemented.

4. Information Sharing

We do not sell customer personal information. We may share relevant information with delivery partners, payment providers or service providers when necessary to fulfil orders and operate our services.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect customer information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration or disclosure. However, no internet-based system can guarantee complete security.

6. Cookies and Website Data

Our website may use browser storage and similar technologies to support features such as shopping carts and wishlists. You can clear browser data through your browser settings.

7. Your Privacy Rights

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements.

8. Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or legal requirements change. The latest version will be published on this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our Contact Us page.
