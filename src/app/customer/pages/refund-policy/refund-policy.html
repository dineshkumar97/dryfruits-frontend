Our Commitment

At RATASREE., we take care to provide quality dry fruits and a good shopping experience. If something is wrong with your order, please contact us so we can review it.

1. Order Cancellation

If you need to cancel an order, contact us as soon as possible with your Order ID and registered mobile number.

Cancellation requests will be reviewed based on the order's processing and dispatch status. An order that has already been dispatched may not be cancellable.

2. Damaged or Incorrect Products

If you receive a damaged, defective or incorrect product, please contact us promptly after delivery.

Please provide your Order ID, a description of the issue and clear photographs of the product and packaging where possible.

3. Return Eligibility

For food safety and hygiene reasons, opened food products may not be eligible for return unless they are defective, damaged, incorrect or otherwise covered by applicable law.

Any return request will be reviewed based on the product condition, order details and applicable consumer rights.

4. Refund Process

Once a refund request is reviewed and approved, we will inform you about the refund method and next steps.

Refunds for prepaid orders, where applicable, will be processed through the original payment method or another mutually agreed lawful method.

5. Refund Timeline

Refund processing time may depend on the payment provider and banking system. We will communicate the expected timeline when your refund is approved.

6. Delivery Charges

Delivery charge refunds, where applicable, will be considered based on the reason for cancellation or return and applicable consumer-protection requirements.

7. How to Request Help

To request a cancellation, return or refund, contact RATASREE. with your Order ID and details of the issue.
